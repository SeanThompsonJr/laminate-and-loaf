What people ask us most

Do you take holiday orders?

Yes. Pies, cakes and pastry boxes for pickup or delivery from December 2 to 24. Christmas Eve orders end at noon.

How much notice do you need?

Three days for pies and pastry boxes, seven days for custom cakes. The order page only shows days that work.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes, from 6 inches up. Choose a flavor and add your message when you order, and we'll email you to confirm.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes: two gluten-free items every day, plus the flourless chocolate torte on the holiday menu. They're made in a kitchen that also uses wheat, so we can't promise there are no traces.

Do you deliver?

Within 5 miles of the shop, for a $6 fee (sample), in the same time windows as pickup.

How do I pay?

When you pick up or when your order arrives, by card or cash.
